Ice dam removal

When ice builds along the roof edge, melting snow backs up under the shingles and can leak into ceilings and walls. We remove it without damaging your roof.

Our process

  1. Inspect the roof edge, gutters, and any interior signs of water.
  2. Clear snow from the lower roof so no more melt feeds the dam.
  3. Remove the ice with low-pressure steam. No picks or hammers that crack shingles.
  4. Open drainage channels and clear gutters and downspouts.
Request ice dam removal

Signs you need us

  1. Thick ice along the eaves or large icicles
  2. Water stains on ceilings or walls near exterior edges
  3. Dripping around window frames or soffits

Please don't chip at ice from a ladder. It's dangerous and it damages roofing.
